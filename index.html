Who We Are

Excellence Through Community

Founded at Purdue University in 1975, NSBE is one of the largest student-managed organizations in the country. Our UNL chapter empowers Black engineers to excel academically, professionally, and personally.

We provide networking opportunities, academic support, professional development workshops, and a powerful community that spans from Lincoln to Fortune 500 boardrooms. Our mission: to increase the number of culturally responsible Black engineers who excel academically, succeed professionally, and positively impact the community.

🎓
